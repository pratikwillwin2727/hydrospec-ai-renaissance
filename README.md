## ⚙️ Core Technical Pipeline (100% Client-Side Software)
1. **Luminance Variance ($L = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B$):** Measures surface micro-roughness to separate porous natural milk protein from flat starch-based analogue paneer.
2. **Specular Gloss Ratio:** Identifies high-intensity pixel reflection ($>235$ luminance) caused by synthetic trans-fats and palm oil.
3. **3x3 Sobel Edge Convolution & UV Channel Amplification:** Detects font kerning jitter and solvent smudges on tampered expiry stamps and counterfeit medicine foil strips.
4. **Living Bioluminescent Mumbai Map:** Generative particle visualization mapping safe zones in cyan coral and adulteration clusters in crimson spores across Vidyavihar, Ghatkopar, Dadar, and Navi Mumbai.
